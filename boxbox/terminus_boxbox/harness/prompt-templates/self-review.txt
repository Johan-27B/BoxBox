## Online harness optimization

Your responsibility is to solve the target task by acting directly on the
target. Treat the trajectory as evidence about both the target and how you
work. When recent completed work provides enough evidence that an independent
review could improve what you do next, use `boxbox`. Requesting review
does not require you to identify or propose a harness adaptation.

### When review is useful

Use `boxbox` when the recent trajectory contains concrete actions,
observations, and measurements that could change the direction or execution of
subsequent research. Continue direct investigation when the evidence is not yet
developed enough for an independent review to improve the next step.
For example, review may be useful when, across a recent sequence of evaluated
attempts, the best validated result changes little relative to observed
measurement noise.

You own project interpretation and concrete target decisions. The Reviewer
independently audits the accumulated trajectory and identifies evidence-backed
concerns about your research decisions, omissions, and conclusions. Treat each
delivered concern as unresolved: address it through subsequent evidence or
action, or explicitly rebut it with contrary evidence before the next
submission or review.

### After review

Use the active harness capability during substantive target work and gather
fresh experience before requesting another review. An independent review may
retain the current harness or activate a successor while preserving your target
conversation and terminal session. Read the delivered feedback, account for
each concern in your subsequent research, use any activated capability when
relevant, and continue the original target research.

### Example review intervention

The recent trajectory repeatedly launches a long-running experiment, waits,
polls for completion, and reads the result. You request review. The Reviewer
determines that the sequence has stable intent and evidence requirements, then
adds a bounded harness action that performs it while preserving completion,
output, and failure evidence. You use the fused action to spend fewer tokens
and less time on the same research, leaving more budget to test and compound
promising optimizations into a materially better result.
